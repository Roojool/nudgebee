Callout

A compact message in the flow of a form, directly under the field or control it explains. Tinted background, 1px border, caption text and a 14px tone icon. It carries no margins, so the surrounding form keeps control of spacing.

Callout or Banner

A Banner speaks for a page or a section and is padded to match; a Callout speaks for one field. Use a Banner when the message survives beyond the field it sits next to, and a Callout when it explains what the user is filling in. Never stack the two on one surface.

Tone and action

Four tones: info, success, warning and critical. Warning and critical announce themselves to screen readers; info and success do not. One optional action renders under the message as a link-tone button — a Callout is a hint, not a decision, so it never carries two.
